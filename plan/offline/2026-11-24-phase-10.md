# Phase 10: offline catch-up

## Goal

When the player comes back, the game credits the time they were away. It gives reduced gains, lets debt keep growing, and puts one return event on the desk. The exit criterion comes from the [development plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/W1LESSbnonaotUx1qEu93J): a save left for a simulated week comes back with reduced gains, undiminished debt and a return event on the desk.

The research is in [[research/offline/2026-11-24-phase-10]], and the work continues [[handoff/prestige/2026-11-24-phase-9-done]].

## Decisions

These come from the development plan's comment thread (2026-10-26) and from Garret on 2026-11-24.

- **Nothing happens while away.** There are no desk events, crown deadlines, decree flips or governor activity. The tick does not advance during catch-up, so every absolute deadline and the season stay as they were at exit.
- **Compressed time.** Time away within a grace window counts in full, and time past it counts logarithmically. Negative or unparseable time away counts as zero.
- **Gains in one step.** Catch-up applies the rates in force at exit, multiplied by the compressed ticks, once. Resources are clamped at zero and at their caps.
- **Season rate.** Catch-up uses the average over the four seasons, not the season at exit.
- **Feeding.** Grain spent on the feed policy goes to growth progress but stops just short of a birth, so nobody is born while away.
- **Illusion and debt.** These use the existing rules: conjured stock fades by `fadePerTick`, and interest is `interestPerSeason` on the original debt. Both run over the **compressed** time, not real time, and no separate offline rate is added.
- **Audits.** The regular rule applies. If the debt crosses the audit threshold, auditors are announced on the first played tick with the usual warning and deadline.
- **Return event.** Its cost is driven by two signals: food (the harvest policy in force sells) and labor (one job takes most of the workers). It scales with the compressed time.

## Proposed defaults

These are low-stakes, so the plan takes them unless Garret objects.

- **Curve.** The new `offline` section of `data/tuning.json` holds `graceTicks` 18,000 (30 minutes) and `curveTicks` 36,000 (1 hour). Compressed time is `grace + curve × log2(1 + (real − grace) / curve)` past the grace window, so:

  | Time away | Compressed time |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1 hour | about 1.1 hours |
  | 8 hours | about 3.6 hours |
  | 1 week | about 7.9 hours |

  The core may not use `Math.log`, so the log2 is a deterministic fixed-point routine in `src/core/math.ts`, next to `powInt`.
- **Return event on a full desk.** Like an audit, it does not count toward the three slots and does not expire. It waits until it is settled.
- **Voice.** When a governor serves, the governor who exaggerates most reports the return, inflated by their report factor unless true reports are owned. Otherwise a clerk reports it, and the clerk's figures are accurate.
- **droppedMs.** Not folded in. The clock comment is rewritten to say that time lost to a stalled frame is dropped on purpose.
- **No schema bump.** Catch-up adds no state fields. The new desk kind is a new union variant, which old saves never contain.

## PRs

The two PRs are sequential. The first one carries the most uncertainty, which is applying the rates in one step, so it goes first.

### 1. `offline-catch-up`: compressed time and catch-up at startup (merged, #44)

Branch from `main`.

- **Tuning.** Add the `offline` section to `data/tuning.json` and its schema in `src/core/tuning.ts`.
- **Math.** Add `log2Fixed` (or a similar deterministic integer log) to `src/core/math.ts`, with unit tests against known values.
- **Core.** Add an `OfflineManager` in `src/core/offline.ts`, reachable as `game.offline` like `game.projection`. Its one entry point is `catchUp(realTicks)`. It returns a summary (compressed ticks, gains per resource, debt added, conjured stock faded) for the notice and for PR 2.
  - **Rates.** Average `ProjectionManager.flows()` over one representative tick in each season. Doing this on a clone with its tick set inside each season reuses the existing projection without a second rate model.
  - **Applying them.** Use `add` with `Math.max(rate × compressed, −current)` for each resource, so stores floor at zero and respect caps. Starvation never runs.
  - **Feeding.** Move fed grain into `economy.growth`, capped just below the birth threshold.
  - **Prospecting.** In an extraction posting, add the ore rate × `prospectingShare` × compressed ticks as coin, because the one-tick projection misses the season-end payout.
  - **Illusion.** Fade conjured stock by `powInt(1 − fade, compressed)`. Charge `principal × rate × compressed / ticksPerSeason` as interest, with the `"interest"` unlock modifier applied.
  - **Ticks.** `state.tick` is not changed.
- **Startup.** `prepareStartup` (`src/tui/app.ts:27-65`) takes an injected `wallNow: () => number`, and `main.ts` passes `Date.now`. After `repealStale`, it reads the time away from `savedAt` and calls `catchUp`, then adds a notice through the existing notice slot that says how long the player was away and what was gained. Every existing `prepareStartup` test passes a fixed `wallNow`.
- **Runner.** `sim.ts` takes `--away <seconds>`, valid only with `--load`, and calls the same `catchUp`.
- **Clock.** Rewrite the `droppedMs` comment in `src/clock.ts:45-47`.
- **Tests** (fast ones only; no long simulations in `bun test`):
  - Compression: zero, negative, inside the grace window, and a week.
  - A week away gives gains below the rate times the real ticks, leaves the tick unchanged, and never lowers debt.
  - Winter at exit still gains grain.
  - Grain floors at zero, and no one starves.
  - Feeding stops just short of a birth.
  - Prospecting is paid only in an extraction posting.
  - Startup with a week-old `savedAt` changes state, and startup with `savedAt` of `"x"` changes nothing.
  - The golden file and the determinism tests stay unchanged.

**Done when:** `bun run check` passes, and a save whose `savedAt` is edited back a week launches under `STEWARD_SAVE_DIR` and shows the return notice with capped stores and more coin.

### 2. `offline-return-event`: the return event on the desk

Branch from `main` (PR 1 merged first).

- **Policies at exit.** `runFlows` returns the `chosen` policies from `DecreeEngine.run` (`src/core/step.ts:24-28`), and `flows()` exposes them so that catch-up knows which policies were in force.
- **Signals.**
  - Food is on when the harvest policy in force is `sell`.
  - Labor is on when one job takes `offline.lopsidedShare` (default 0.8) or more of the allocation.
- **Scale.** `scale = signals × compressed hours × offline.returnScalePerHour`, capped at `offline.returnScaleMax`. With no signals, the event is a report whose options cost nothing.
- **Desk kind.** Add a `return` kind to `DESK_KINDS` but not to `DRAWN_KINDS`. It gets a variant in the `DeskEntry` union and schema, a kind in `deskTuningSchema` with its placeholders (hours away, the gains, and the reporter's name), templates in `data/desk.json`, a branch in `fill`, and copy.
  - It is placed without randomness, the way `placeAudit` does (`desk.ts:574-589`), with figures stored on the entry so that a reload shows the same text.
  - It is excluded from the slot count and from expiry, like an audit.
- **Voice.** The reporting governor is chosen with the ordering `dismissAfterFailedAudit` uses (`governors.ts:418-432`). The reported gain is inflated by that governor's report factor unless `precision.reports` is owned.
- **Wiring.** `catchUp` places the entry when the compressed ticks are above zero.
- **Tests.**
  - The exit-criterion test: build a village with conjuring and debt, catch up a week, and assert reduced gains, debt at least as high as before, and one `return` entry on the desk.
  - Pairs: no signals against both signals, governor voice against clerk voice, and a full desk keeping all three entries.
  - The golden hash is unchanged.

**Done when:** `bun run check` passes, and the manual week-old save shows the return event on the desk before the first key press.

## Risks

- **Debt still grows fast on compressed time.** A season is one real minute, so a week away charges about 474 seasons of interest, roughly 24 times the original debt. An hour away charges about 3 times. Any unbacked debt left at exit almost certainly ends in a failed audit after the return. Garret chose the regular rule for now. Revisit it in the post-phase playtest, with `curveTicks` or the interest rate as the levers.
- **Gains are mostly coin.** Stores reach their caps in minutes, so after a long absence the visible gain is coin from selling decrees. That is fine, but the notice should say which stores were full.

## Review patterns

- **Load-time transform, not a command.** Catch-up runs at load like `repealStale`. It is not applied in `step`, and it stays out of `parseSave` and `loadGame`, so replays and the determinism tests are unaffected.
- **No `Date` or `Math.log` in `src/core`.** The wall clock is injected at the TUI or runner boundary.
- **No long simulations in `bun test`.**
- **Data changes move the golden hash.** If it moves, run biome on `test/golden.json`.
- **Code comments are why-only.** Plan wording must not leak into code.
