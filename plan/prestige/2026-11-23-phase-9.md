# Phase 9: prestige, from the village into a second posting

## Goal

A career runs from the village into a second posting. This is the exit from the [development plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/W1LESSbnonaotUx1qEu93J). The posting ends when the player chooses. Career unlocks, saved decrees and loyal governors carry over. Resources, population, posting-scoped Archive purchases and illusion debt reset. The player commits to a doctrine, and the new province has rules of its own.

The research is in [[research/prestige/2026-11-23-phase-9]].

## Decisions (Garret, 2026-11-23)

1. **The player ends the posting.** Ending is offered once a milestone is reached. The crown favor band at that moment names the ending:
   - warm is a promotion
   - neutral is a reassignment
   - cold is a scandal

   The ending sets how good the next province is. A forced exile waits for a later phase.
2. **Archive entries are split by scope.**
   - Panels, controls, decree features, precision, petitions and omens persist.
   - Stores, tools, `market.rights`, `lenders.favor`, `court.friends`, `governors.physician` and `governors.slots` reset.
   - Reset entries stay revealed and are bought again at the same price.
3. **Loyal governors follow, up to a cap.** Each governor gets a loyalty score. At the posting's end, the most loyal governors above a threshold follow, up to 2. Rivals wait.
4. **A doctrine is a bundle of modifiers plus one rule.** Trade, agriculture and extraction each change existing modifiers and add one mechanic. The province is a bundle of modifiers plus a factor that escalates crown demands and audits.

## Proposed defaults

These are not yet confirmed. All numbers live in `data/prestige.json`.

- **Milestone.** The posting can end once 6 crown demands have been met in it. The Crown panel says so when it becomes true.
- **Crown favor resets to 0** in the new posting. The ending and province are where favor pays off. The career keeps a history: for each posting, its name, ending, final favor, doctrine and province.
- **How posting 2 starts.**
  - Already promoted. The Crown charter is owned, since it is a career entry.
  - The first crown demand is drawn from the usual `firstDemand` range, starting from the posting's start tick.
  - 12 peasants, plus any followers serving as governors.
  - Starting stores of grain 200, wood 60 and ore 20.
  - The player names the province with the existing name prompt. The runner and replays use a name drawn from the seed.
- **Seed.** Posting N's seed is a fixed hash of the previous posting's seed and the posting index, so replay stays deterministic.
- **The tick stays global.** A new posting stores `startedAt = tick`, and nothing is rebased.
- **Escalation.** Crown base amounts and the audit debt share are multiplied by `1 + 0.25 × (posting index − 1)`, times the province's factor.
- **Provinces.** A promotion draws two provinces and takes the kinder one. A scandal draws from the harsh list.

  | Province | Modifiers | Demand and audit factor |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Fertile valley | farm yield ×1.15 | 1.0 |
  | Timber march | forest yield ×1.2, grain cap ×0.9 | 1.05 |
  | Poor soil (harsh) | farm yield ×0.8 | 1.1 |
  | Entrenched nobles (harsh) | audit gap ×0.8, interest ×1.25 | 1.15 |

- **Doctrines.** Each has one bonus, one cost and one rule. The rules are the part most worth Garret's eye.

  | Doctrine | Modifiers | Rule |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Trade | sell price ×1.25, farm yield ×0.9 | **Crown accepts coin.** A grain, wood or ore demand can be paid in coin at the market price. |
  | Agriculture | farm yield ×1.2, grain cap ×1.25, mine yield ×0.8 | **Full granaries feed growth.** Grain lost to the cap goes to the next peasant's progress instead. |
  | Extraction | mine and forest yield ×1.2, farm yield ×0.85 | **Prospecting.** Each season end pays coin equal to a share of the ore mined that season. |

- **Loyalty.**
  - It starts at 0.
  - It rises 1 for each season served, and 3 when the governor serves through a backed audit.
  - It falls 5 when the crown misses a demand while the governor serves.
  - It drops to 0 when the governor's hoard is seized.
  - A governor follows at loyalty 6 or more, so roughly a full term of service.
  - Followers keep their name, traits and loyalty. They get a new id, a new term, and a village in the new posting.

### 1. `prestige-foundation`: ending a posting and starting the next

This has the most uncertainty, so it goes first.

- **Core.**
  - Extract `newPosting(seed, name, tuning, start)` from `Game.create` (`src/core/game.ts:75-128`). `Game.create` then calls it for posting 1.
  - Add `scope: "career" | "posting"` to Archive entries (`data/archive.json`, plus its schema in `src/core/unlocks.ts`). The default is career, and the posting-scoped list is the one in decision 2.
  - Add a `PrestigeManager` in `src/core/prestige.ts`. It owns:
    - the milestone check
    - the ending, read from `CrownManager.band`
    - seed derivation
    - the province draw, with only a province id in this PR; effects come in PR 2
    - building the next posting: drop posting-scoped unlocks, keep `seen`, schedule the first demand, apply the starting stores and population
  - Add an `endPosting { name }` command applied in `step`, so replay holds. Core refuses it before the milestone. Unlike panel gates, this is a game rule, not a screen gate.
  - The career gains `postings: PostingRecord[]`. The posting gains `index` and `startedAt`. Remove `postingId`, which is always `"posting-1"`, or derive it from `index`.
  - Schema 18 upgrade: existing saves become posting 1 with an empty history.
- **TUI.**
  - When the milestone is reached, a notice like `promotion-notice.ts` appears.
  - A key on the Crown panel opens the posting-end screen. It is a `menu.ts` screen that shows the ending, what carries over and what resets, then asks for the name with `name-prompt.ts` and issues `endPosting`.
  - The Ledger title line shows the posting number.
- **Runner.**
  - Add an `endPosting` setup step or reaction, gated on the milestone.
  - Purchases skip entries that are owned and buy again those that were reset.
  - Setup can be declared for each posting, for example `postings: [{setup, purchases}, ...]`, with the last entry repeating.
  - `sim.ts` reports outcomes for each posting.
- **Tests.**
  - `prestige.test.ts`:
    - Posting-scoped entries reset, while career entries and `seen` persist.
    - The ending follows the favor band.
    - The milestone gate refuses early ends and allows ends once reached.
    - The derived seed is deterministic.
  - Save upgrade 17 to 18.
  - Exit invariant in `strategies.test.ts`: `reference` plays from the village into posting 2 and meets a demand there. This is a progress invariant only, with no timing comparisons.
  - Golden update. Run `bun run golden:update`, then `bunx biome check --write test/golden.json`. The golden command log should include an `endPosting`.
- **Done when:** `bun run check` passes, and a TUI run reaches posting 2 from a saved posting-1 game at the milestone.

### 2. `prestige-doctrines`: doctrines, province effects and escalation

- **Doctrines and provinces as data.** Add `data/doctrines.json` and province entries in `data/prestige.json`. Both use the `ArchiveEffects` shape and are validated in `src/save/content.ts`.
- **Modifier sources.** `modifier()` (`src/core/unlocks.ts:141-150`) also folds in the effects of the posting's `doctrine` and `province`. The posting stores both ids.
- **The command.** `endPosting` gains `doctrine`.
- **The three rules:**
  - Trade: `CrownManager` lets a resource demand be paid in coin at `sellPrice`.
  - Agriculture: in `economy.ts`, overflow at the grain cap goes to growth progress. `add()` already knows when an amount is lost to the cap through `AddContext.capOf`.
  - Extraction: a season-end coin payout in `economy.seasonEnds`.
- **Escalation.** The demand base in `crown.ts:300-315` and the audit share in `illusion.ts:494-527` are multiplied by the posting factor.
- **TUI.**
  - The posting-end screen gains a doctrine step, a list showing each doctrine's bonus, cost and rule.
  - The Ledger shows the doctrine and province.
  - `GameView.live` already shows the adjusted figures.
- **Tests:**
  - Each rule, with its positive and negative case.
  - Modifiers stack across the Archive, the doctrine and the province.
  - A progress check that posting 2's first purchase is affordable under every doctrine and province pairing.

### 3. `prestige-followers`: loyalty and governors who follow

- **Loyalty.** The `Governor` type (`src/core/delegates.ts:17-24`) gains `loyalty`. `GovernorManager` changes it on season end, backed audits, missed demands and seized hoards.
- **Following.** At `endPosting`, the top 2 governors at or above the threshold go to `career.followers`, which changes from `string[]` to `Follower[]`.
- **Arriving.** The next posting seats them as governors with new ids and terms. A name that clashes with `village.names` is skipped when peasants are drawn.
- **Departures.** A governor who leaves keeps their loyalty in `formerStaff`.
- **Schema 19.** Unless PR 1's schema bump is still unmerged, in which case this folds into 18.
- **TUI.**
  - Governor rows show loyalty as a word: wary, steady or devoted.
  - The posting-end screen lists who will follow.
- **Tests:**
  - Each loyalty driver.
  - Follower selection respects the threshold and the cap.
  - Followers arrive seated, with new terms.
  - A name clash doesn't duplicate a name.

## Review patterns

- **Core enforces game rules.** The posting-end milestone is a game rule, so core enforces it. Panel and command gates stay TUI-only so that old logs replay.
- **No comparative strategy tests.** Assert progress invariants only.
- **Data changes move the golden hash.** Always run biome on `test/golden.json` after updating it.
- **Code comments are why-only.** Plan wording must not leak into code.
