# Phase 10 merged: offline catch-up and the return report

## Context

This continues [[plan/offline/2026-11-24-phase-10]], which is now completed. The research is in [[research/offline/2026-11-24-phase-10]], and the work follows [[handoff/prestige/2026-11-24-phase-9-done]].

Phase 10 is the last phase of the [development plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/W1LESSbnonaotUx1qEu93J). All of it is on `main` at bf74c6a. The save schema is still version 20, because catch-up adds no state fields.

## What we built

### Catch-up at load

- **Where it runs.** `prepareStartup` in `src/tui/app.ts` takes an injected `wallNow`, and `main.ts` passes `Date.now`. After `repealStale`, it reads the save's `savedAt` and calls `game.offline.catchUp(realTicks)`.
  - `OfflineManager.ticksAway` converts milliseconds to ticks. It treats an unparseable or future `savedAt` as zero time away.
  - Catch-up is a load-time transform like `repealStale`. It is not a `step` command, and it is not inside `parseSave` or `loadGame`.
- **Nothing happens while away.** The tick does not advance, so every absolute deadline and the season stay as they were at exit. No desk events, crown deadlines, governor activity, starvation or births run.
- **Compressed time.** Up to `offline.graceTicks` counts in full: 18,000 ticks, which is 30 minutes. Past that, time counts as `curveTicks × log2(1 + extra / curveTicks)`, with `curveTicks` at 36,000 ticks (1 hour). The log is `log2Fixed` in `src/core/math.ts`, because `Math.log` is banned in the core.

  | Time away | Counts as |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1 hour | about 1.1 hours |
  | 8 hours | about 3.6 hours |
  | 1 week | about 7.9 hours |
- **Gains.** Catch-up averages `projection.flows(fromTick)` over one mid-season tick in each of the four seasons, then applies each rate once over the compressed ticks with `add`. Stores floor at zero and respect their caps.
  - Grain that the feed policy spends fills `economy.growth`, stopping just short of a birth.
  - An extraction posting earns prospecting coin on the gross ore dug (`oreDugPerTick`), the same figure live play pays on, so a full ore store still earns it.
- **Illusion and debt.** `IllusionManager.elapse(ticks)` fades conjured stock and charges interest with the existing per-season rule, over the compressed ticks. Debt never goes down.
- **Startup notice.** It says how long the player was away, what was gained (through `formatGains` in `src/core/amount.ts`) and which stores were full.
- **Runner.** `sim.ts --load <save> --away <seconds>` runs the same catch-up.

### The return report

- **Desk kind.** A `return` entry is placed at load whenever the compressed time is above zero, before the player can act.
  - It is placed without randomness by `DeskManager.place`, which it shares with audits. It is not a drawn kind, so the golden hash is unchanged.
  - It takes no desk slot and never expires. A second catch-up replaces an unanswered report rather than stacking a second one.
- **Cost.** The scale is `signals × compressed hours × returnScalePerHour` (0.5), capped at `returnScaleMax` (12). The two signals are:
  - **Food:** the harvest policy in force at exit is `sell`.
  - **Labor:** the labor allocation in force (`game.labor.policy`) puts at least `lopsidedShare` (0.8) of the workers on one job.

  With no signals, the options cost nothing. A week away with both signals costs about 237 coin, or 276 grain plus 118 wood.
- **Voice.** `GovernorManager.leadingReporter` picks the governor who exaggerates most, the same ordering a failed-audit dismissal uses. The reported gains are inflated by that governor's report factor unless `precision.reports` is owned. With no governor, a clerk reports accurate figures.

## Gotchas

- **Inject the wall clock.** `Date` is banned in `src/core`. Every `prepareStartup` test must pass a fixed `wallNow`, or fixture saves with old `savedAt` values trigger catch-up.
- **Catch-up stays out of `parseSave` and `loadGame`.** Putting it there would break the split-run determinism test, because resuming would apply real wall time.
- **Rates come from the projection.** `flows()` measures the change after caps, so a store already at its cap has a rate near zero for that store. Offline gains never overshoot, but they understate a store that was full at exit. Prospecting is the exception: it uses the gross ore dug.
- **Return reports and audits differ on slots.** Audits take a slot but are never pushed out. Return reports take no slot. The desk's overflow loop checks the two separately on purpose.
- **The return template can't be free.** The desk schema requires every template to have an expiry option that gains nothing, so both options cost something whenever the scale is above zero. A poor village may not afford either for a while; the entry waits and blocks nothing.
- **Time lost to frozen frames is dropped.** The clock's `droppedMs` is not routed to catch-up. The comment in `src/clock.ts` says why.
