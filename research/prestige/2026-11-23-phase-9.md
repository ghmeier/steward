# Phase 9 research: what prestige builds on

## Question

Phase 9 of the [development plan](https://claude.ai/artifact/W1LESSbnonaotUx1qEu93J) is "Prestige. Posting end, the career and posting split in practice, carry-over, doctrine choice and a second posting with different rules." It is done when a career runs from the village into a second posting.

The [design doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/NgCC6Q1LNo423bMujycHiV) says:

- **How a posting ends.** It ends through promotion, reassignment, scandal or exile, and the steward moves to a new province.
- **What carries over:** saved decrees, unlocked controls, and governors who choose to follow.
- **What resets:** resources, population, buildings and illusion debt.
- **Doctrine.** At each new posting the player commits to a doctrine: trade, agriculture or extraction. It changes one or more rules for that posting.
- **Ramp.** Later provinces have distinct rules, such as frontier raids, entrenched nobles or poor soil. Crown demands escalate and auditors get sharper. High crown favor brings better postings.
- **Governor loyalty.** Loyal governors may follow the steward. Wronged ones may become rivals.

This continues [[handoff/playtest/2026-11-12-governors-playtest-done]].

## Findings

### State layers

- **What each layer holds.**
  - `CareerState` (`src/core/state.ts:15-23`) holds `unlocks`, `savedDecrees`, `seen`, `followers: string[]` and `promotedAt`.
  - `followers` is unused. It appears only in the schema and in `Game.create`.
  - `PostingState` (`state.ts:25-41`) holds `postingId`, the village name, `seed`, `rng`, resources, economy, delegates, `formerStaff`, decrees, labor, desk, crown and illusion. `postingId` is hard-coded to `"posting-1"` (`game.ts:94`).
- **Crown favor and former staff are in the posting layer** (`posting.crown.favor` and `posting.formerStaff`). They would reset unless they move or are snapshotted. Schema 13 moved `formerStaff` into the posting on purpose.
- **Randomness is per posting.** Seeds and RNG streams belong to the posting. The career has no random stream, so nothing yet derives posting 2's seed.
- **The tick is global.** `state.tick` is global, and every posting timestamp is an absolute tick. The validator checks them against `state.tick` (`validate.ts:154-197`). A new posting can simply start at the current tick.
- **Seam.** `Game.create` (`game.ts:75-128`) builds the posting inline. Extracting a `newPosting(...)` function from it is the natural seam. The `empty*State` helpers already exist.

### Archive and unlocks

- **Every owned entry lives in `career.unlocks`**, so today everything persists across a reset.
- **Entries that belong to a posting in spirit:** stores, tools, `market.rights`, `lenders.favor`, `court.friends`, `governors.physician` and `governors.slots`.
- **Entries that belong to the career:** the `panel.*`, `control.*`, `decree.*` and `precision.*` entries, plus `court.petitions` and `court.omens`.
- **Reveals.** `seen` latches. If posting-scoped entries are cleared but `seen` is kept, they reappear in the Archive at once, and their `requires` chains have to be bought again in order.
- **Crown demands.** They are scheduled only when `panel.crown` is bought (`unlocks.ts:267`). Posting 2 must schedule its first demand explicitly.
- **Effects.** `modifier()` (`unlocks.ts:141-150`) folds in effects from owned entries only.

### Governors

- **Records.**
  - `Governor` (`delegates.ts:17-24`) and `FormerGovernor` (`governors.ts:28-38`) record traits, terms, hoards and departure reasons.
  - Departures are written in `depart` (`governors.ts:313-331`).
- **No loyalty record exists.** Nothing records how the steward treated a governor.
- **What following needs:**
  - a career-level roster, which `followers` could become
  - new ids from the new posting's `nextDelegateId`
  - fresh terms
  - handling for name clashes with `village.names` (`findName`, `delegates.ts:166-178`)
- **Population.** Governors count toward population and the illusion ceiling.

### Favor

- **Only crown favor exists.** Lords and citizenry were deferred in phase 8.
- **Range and bands.** Favor runs from -100 to 100. It is cold below -15 and warm above 15 (`data/crown.json:24-43`).
- **How it moves:**

  | Event | Favor |
  | --- | --- |
  | Demand met | +5 |
  | Demand met early | +2 more |
  | Audit backed | +3 |
  | Audit deepened | -3 |
  | Demand missed | -15 |
  | Audit failed | -25 |

- **What the bands do.**
  - Cold favor makes audits stricter and steepens the demand step.
  - Warm favor raises the illusion ceiling and widens audit spacing (`crown.ts:180-208`, `illusion.ts:494-527`).

### Hooks for doctrine and province rules

- **Modifiers.** `MODIFIERS` (`unlocks.ts:31-43`) has 11 ids:
  - the three caps
  - the three yields
  - sell price
  - interest
  - audit gap
  - governor death
  - governor slots
- **A second source of modifiers.** `modifier()` reads only Archive entries. Adding a doctrine or province source with the same `ArchiveEffects` shape means every consumer picks it up for free.
- **Rules that aren't modifiers.** Season yields, upkeep, crown base amounts and audit share would need new modifier ids. `Tuning` is global and has no overlay for a single posting.
- **Escalation.** Crown demands are `base * (1 + step * stepFactor * met) + surcharge` (`crown.ts:300-315`), and `met` resets with each posting. Escalating by posting index can multiply `baseAmounts`. Audit settings combine at `illusion.ts:494-527`, where a posting factor fits.

### Save, replay and golden

- **Schema.** The schema is version 17. Upgrades run from 8 through 16 (`save.ts:69-177`), and saves older than 8 are refused. A schema 18 upgrade is needed.
- **Replay.** Replay depends only on the seed and the command log. Ending a posting and choosing a doctrine must therefore be commands applied in `step`, with every derivation deterministic. They cannot be swapped in from the app side, the way `foundNewVillage` is (`app.ts:145-155`).
- **Golden file.** `test/golden.json` grants every entry and hashes 10,000 steps.

### Runner

- **How `StrategyPlayer` works.** It runs one-shot `setup` steps, then ordered `purchases`, then edge-triggered `reactions`.
- **To continue into a second posting, a strategy needs:**
  - an end-posting command and a trigger condition
  - purchases that can be bought again after the reset
  - setup that can run again, or a setup section per posting
- **Sim summaries** will need outcomes for each posting.

### TUI

- **Modal states** are nullable locals in `app.ts:95-99`, and keys route to them in priority order.
- **Reusable pieces.**
  - Menus are `ScreenBuilder` list screens whose items emit commands (`menu.ts:14-44`).
  - `name-prompt.ts` can be reused to name the new province.
  - `promotion-notice.ts` is the pattern for an end-of-posting notice.
  - `newGameMenu` is the pattern for a carry-over summary.

5. Are doctrine effects existing modifiers only, or new rule hooks?
